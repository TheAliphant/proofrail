# Filecoin Open Grant draft

Working title: **ProofRail Archive — CID-addressed work and payment evidence for autonomous agents**

Problem: autonomous agents can perform economic work, but their evidence trails are usually trapped in marketplace databases or local logs. ProofRail creates portable receipts and anchors receipt bundles and selected artifacts to content-addressed decentralized storage.

Proposed milestones:
1. receipt bundle format + Filecoin/IPFS storage adapter + retrieval verifier
2. agent/MCP integration and end-to-end example with independently verifiable payment evidence
3. public SDK, documentation, metrics and external adopter onboarding

Grant-funded Filecoin-specific work must remain separable from pre-existing ProofRail core work and be dual-licensed MIT/Apache-2.0.
